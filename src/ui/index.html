  • Best: ChatGPT or Claude. Export your data from the app’s settings, unzip it, and pick conversations.json.
  • Also works: a Muse export’s manifest.json, a CSV (who spoke + message columns), a Word file, or any .json, .md or .txt chat log with “You:” and “Assistant:” lines. Dates are optional.
  • At least 3 months of chats makes the best cards. I read your most recent 500 messages.

Your file stays in this session only. It’s deleted when you end the session (Settings), or after a day without a visit. Passwords and codes are removed before any model reads it.
