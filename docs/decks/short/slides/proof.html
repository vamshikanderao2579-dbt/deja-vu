Does it work? We tested three ways an agent can remember you. The main set is four people: two of us, with our own real chat history, and two synthetic people, 76 checks judged by Muse Spark. Re-reading the full history got 74 percent right, a memory summary 79, and Déjà Vu memory 88. When life changed in ways that never came up in chat, Déjà Vu got 92 percent; the others, 42 and 58. It reads about 150 tokens of memory per message. Then ten more synthetic people, each a week of chats, 236 checks. Their first run showed thin builds on two of them, so we fixed the builder: a second pass for missed facts, and a grader that rewords instead of rejecting. Now Déjà Vu gets 80 percent against 69.5, and 81 against 49 on changes outside chat, best or tied for 13 of 14 people. Since that fix came after reading two of their builds, we don't call them fully held out.
