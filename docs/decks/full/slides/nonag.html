The risk with asking is nagging. So there’s a strict rule, and it’s plain code, not a model guess. The agent only asks when all four are true: the card actually changes this answer, it’s unsure about it, the stakes are real, and it hasn’t asked recently. Most conversations get no question at all. It’s plain code, not a model guess, so it’s predictable. And when you just keep chatting after a check, that counts as a quiet yes; two of them confirm the card.
