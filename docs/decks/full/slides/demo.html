One reminder before the demo: this isn’t a chatbot. The chat is just a host agent called Pip; Déjà Vu is the memory layer inside it. Switch to the live link and press Watch the 2-minute demo on the first page; choose Captions only and talk over it, or let the voice narrate. Beat 1: one thing Déjà Vu remembers about Alex is out of date. Beat 2: Alex asks for a hotel near Sunset Cliffs; the answer is built from a few cards, about 150 tokens instead of the whole history, and one line checks the guess that shaped it: hotels, not Airbnbs. Tap the card and it opens the June chat it came from, when an Airbnb cancelled. Beat 3: that changed, so tap ✕ yourself, then one tap says what changed: an Airbnb with a kitchen is fine now. Beat 4: the pick switches to an Airbnb, with what’s new and what’s gone marked, and nothing booked without Alex. It ends on Memory and three buttons: try it yourself, open Memory, see the numbers. Then come back to the slides.
