So to close. Agents are really good at inferring context from chats, and they should keep doing that. But confirmed memory is always better than inferred memory. Give people the key to their own context, ask once at the right moment, and you get an agent people trust, that stays accurate for years, and that’s affordable enough to be free for everyone.
