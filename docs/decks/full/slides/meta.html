13 / 15

Every Meta model here has one job, picked for that job. Muse Spark 1.3 writes the answers, picks the cards each request needs, and makes the drafts, because it’s agent-ready: tool calls come back as structured drafts, and it’s fast at low reasoning. Muse Wonder is the grader: a different, stronger model checks every card Muse Spark writes, so the model that writes a card never approves it. Cards are fourteen words at most, so a batch costs under a cent. Muse Voice Transcribe lets you answer a check by voice, hands-free, ready for glasses. Next: the background memory work and the locked sensitive cards move to Meta’s open weights, Muse Spark Open or Glimmer, so the most personal processing runs on weights you control. After that, SAM 3.1 and vision turn a photo of a ticket or receipt into cards, and Muse Wonder also becomes the independent judge for our eval.
