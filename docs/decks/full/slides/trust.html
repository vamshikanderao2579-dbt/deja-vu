Trust comes from receipts. Every card links back to the exact conversation it came from. Sensitive things like health or money are marked private: used carefully, checked once when they shape something that matters, and yours to remove any time. Confirming is never a gate in front of memory. And the whole memory is a portable file you can take to any agent. To be clear about what this is not: it’s not for ads, not for segmenting people, not for selling data. User-owned is the point.
