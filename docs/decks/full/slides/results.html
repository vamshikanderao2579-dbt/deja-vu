Does it work? We tested three ways an agent can remember you, on fourteen people: two of us, with our own real chat history, and twelve synthetic people, 312 checks judged by Muse Spark, each counted once. Re-reading the full history got 70.5 percent right, a memory summary 71.8, and Déjà Vu memory 81.7. When life changed in ways that never came up in chat, Déjà Vu got 82.3 percent; the others, 49 and 48. It reads about 150 tokens of memory per message, against up to 39 thousand for the full history. Déjà Vu is best or tied for 13 of the 14 people; it loses on one. To be honest about the method: ten of the synthetic people were written after the engine was built, and we fixed the builder after reading two of their builds, so they are not fully held out. Their first, untouched run was 72.5 percent against 69.5.
